## 1.00 Public Release
The first public release of ModelVault: a home for your 3D printing hobby that runs on your own Windows PC or NAS.

### Models
- Add ZIP, 7Z and RAR archives (including split archives), STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP, pictures, videos, PDFs or whole folders. Check each one in Imports, then add it to your library.
- Archives stay zipped to save space; look inside them, preview their 3D files and open them in your slicer.
- 3D viewer for STL, 3MF and OBJ with print dimensions, plus picture, video and PDF viewers.
- Folder naming you choose, with a sample as you type, for new imports or your whole library.
- Duplicate checks, pasted covers, categories, tags, favourites, search and filters, and tiles, list and folder views.
- Watch folders pick up new downloads by themselves.
- Opens OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer and Lychee straight from ModelVault.

### Paints
- About 6,000 paints from 10+ brands, with matches across brands, shades and highlights, palettes from pictures, paint schemes for each model, racks and a shopping list.

### Materials
- Your filament spools and resin bottles, picked from 6,383 filaments and 309 resins, with colour and multi-colour search.
- A material guide for every kind of filament and resin, checked against your printers.

### Tools
- Your printers, nozzles, airbrushes, brushes and more, picked from about 600 printers, with service reminders and history.

### Looking after your library
- Backups with or without model files, and restoring on Windows or a NAS.
- Import an existing library from Orynt3D, Manyfold, Thingiverse downloads or any folder.
- Updates from Settings > About, with an optional daily check.

### Windows
- An installer and a portable version, with Node.js and 7-Zip included.
- An icon next to the clock to open, start, stop and restart ModelVault, view its log and start it with Windows.

### Getting around
- A short guided tour for new users, and Settings > Tailscale with a QR code to open ModelVault on your tablet or phone.
